What is Retrac?

Retrac is a free, community-driven project and custom launcher that lets players relive classic Fortnite Chapter 2 Season 4 (v14.40 / Stark Season) with authentic OG movement, original loot pools, and dedicated servers worldwide.

Which version of Fortnite does Retrac support?

Retrac specifically restores Fortnite Chapter 2 Season 4 (v14.40), bringing back the original Stark Season map, iconic weapons, vehicle mechanics, and classic gameplay.

How do I download and install the Retrac launcher?

Click 'Download Retrac for Free' above to get the official installer. Run the setup to install required files, connect your Discord account once for authentication, and jump directly into matchmaking.

Is Retrac safe to use?

Yes. Retrac holds an EV Code Signing Certificate issued by DigiCert and is manually checked by Microsoft Security Analysis before each update to ensure safety and integrity.

Is Retrac free to play?

Yes, Retrac is 100% free and community-driven. All matchmaking, servers, and classic seasonal gameplay are available without any purchase.

Is Retrac affiliated with Epic Games?

No. Retrac is an independent fan-made community project created for legacy gameplay preservation and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Games, Inc.
